Connect this to the lecture’s five-rating example

The lecture uses quality, helpfulness, clarity, easiness, and rater interest. Its PC1 mainly follows quality, helpfulness, and clarity together. Its PC2 mainly follows easiness relative to the other ratings.

The two-rating picture above makes the projection visible on a page. Its loadings and variance percentages come from a separate PCA of quality and easiness. The same ideas apply to the full five-rating analysis.

Lecture PC1: overall ratings

Quality, helpfulness, and clarity have similar loading magnitudes, about 0.53. PC1 captures 78.2% of the five-rating variation.

Lecture PC2: easiness

Easiness has a loading of +0.92, with smaller negative loadings for the other ratings. PC2 captures another 12.6%.

In five dimensions, removing PC1 leaves several possible patterns. PC2 is the strongest pattern in that remaining variation.
