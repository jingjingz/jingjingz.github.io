The OLS formula

β̂₁ = rxy SDy / SDx
β̂₀ = ȳ − β̂₁x̄

For varying X, β̂₁ = SXY / SXX. The correlation form also requires varying Y.

With an intercept

The OLS line passes through (x̄, ȳ), and its residuals sum to zero. These are numerical properties of the fit.

Error versus residual

An error is the gap from the population mean. A residual is the observed gap from a fitted line. In real data, the population line is unknown.
