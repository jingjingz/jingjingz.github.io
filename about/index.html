About

Jingjing Zou, PhD

Associate Professor in Biostatistics · UC San Diego

I am a faculty member of Moores Cancer Center and co-leader of the Data Management and Statistics Core at the UC San Diego Shiley-Marcos Alzheimer’s Disease Research Center.

I am a statistician working on machine learning and statistical methods for human health. My research spans self-supervised learning for wearable sensors, functional and longitudinal data analysis, and medical imaging.

I am particularly interested in what models learn from complex observations, how they generalize across people and settings, and how to assess findings before applying them to scientific or clinical questions.

Our projects include links to papers, code, and interactive demonstrations.
