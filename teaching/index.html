Biostatistical Methods I

A core graduate course in applied regression for biostatistics students, taught in R. These interactive demonstrations accompany the lectures. Change a model, move a point, or change how data go missing, and watch the fit, its uncertainty, and its interpretation respond.

Teaching and mentoring

I developed and taught Special Topics in Nonparametric Statistics and Functional Data Analysis and have taught Advanced Topics in Biostatistical Inference. I mentor PhD and master's students in biostatistics, statistics, data science, and computer science on projects in wearable sensing, functional data analysis, medical imaging, and AI.
