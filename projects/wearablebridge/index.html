03WearableBridge
Preprint · 2026

Can richer sensors and lab data teach a wrist device in everyday life?

WearableBridge learns a small map from free-living wrist features into the activity regions of a fixed reference model. Richer recordings guide the map, while prediction uses the wrist signal alone.

Jingjing Zou, Yixin Wang
