02W-JEPA
NeurIPS BrainBodyFM Workshop · 2026

How can one model learn across sensors, devices, and sampling rates?

W-JEPA puts recordings on a common time scale and organizes motion into wavelet tokens by scale, sensor axis, and time. Predicting masked token representations teaches the model the structure shared across these inputs.

Yogesh Prabhu, Loki Natarajan, Jingjing Zou
