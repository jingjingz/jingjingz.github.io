How can sensor streams learn from one another without labels?

MoCA connects cross-modal masking to kernel canonical correlation analysis, revealing how masking shapes the shared structure a model learns. One modality helps reconstruct another, turning their correlations into supervision.

Howon Ryu, Yuliang Chen, et al.
