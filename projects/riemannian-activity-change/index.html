04Riemannian activity change
Annals of Applied Statistics · 2023

How do we measure a change in the shape of someone’s day?

We model daily activity curves as smooth deformations that capture changes in timing and amount together. These transformations give us a way to compare how people’s activity patterns change over time.

Jingjing Zou, Tuo Lin, et al.
